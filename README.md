 A Smart Public Transport Tracking and Demand Prediction System
 
“Pothe” (“পথে”)  is a web-based ecosystem where human checkers, passengers, bus owners and gov. share central source of information  about where the buses are, how crowded they are and where the highest demand is. The system estimates how far the bus is from the passengers and tells which buses they can wait for. It turns the collected data into reports for the owners and policy makers.
